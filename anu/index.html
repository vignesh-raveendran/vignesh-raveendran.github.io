Can I steal you away with me?

Anu,

Can I steal you away from 6:00 PM on August 14 until 6:00 PM on August 15?

I want to remember them by the little things. The conversations that go on longer than we planned, the laughs that come out of nowhere, the quiet moments where we don't even need to say anything.

More than a perfect day, I just want a day that feels like us.
So, what do I have in mind?
You & me
A little Bangalore
Good food
Long conversations
Random walks
Favourite songs
Lots of pictures
Little memories

I’ll pick you up along with the sunset that welcomes us and see that smile of yours in person. I want to take you somewhere nice for dinner, sit beside you and let one conversation turn into five because neither of us wants it to end.

Those random walks where the destination doesn't matter. Just the two of us walking together and talking about everything and nothing. Later we can watch our comfort movie together with munchies and then sit at the terrace gazing at the stars while the night quietly keeps us company.

And when the next morning comes, I want us to take it slowly. Breakfast together. A little wandering around. Too many pictures. Something delicious to eat. Maybe a dessert we said we would share and somehow end up fighting over.

Little pieces of happiness that we can keep for ourselves.

“Let the streets of Bangalore keep our laughter,
let the night keep our conversations
and let these 24 hours become one of those memories
we smile about without even realizing it.”

Because I don't really care where we go. If I'm with you, I already know I'll be exactly where I want to be.

And maybe that's what I am really asking for.

Your time. Your palms and red bag in mine. Your smile beside me. Your company.

For one little day, let me take you away from the usual routine of this world. Let me make you laugh a little more and make you feel a little more safer and loved while giving you a day where you don't have to think about anything except enjoying yourself.

I can't promise that every minute will be perfect. But I can promise that I'll be there, paying attention to the little things, because when it comes to you those little things matter to me.

Will you say yes?

Your’s Poetically,
Rohan
